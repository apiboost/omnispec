# Apiboost Trademark Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how the trademarks owned by Apiboost, Inc. ("Apiboost") may
and may not be used. Its goals are to (a) let the community accurately refer to
Apiboost OmniSpec and related products, while (b) preventing uses that would
confuse users about the source, sponsorship, or endorsement of software or
services.

The source code of the open-source core of Apiboost OmniSpec is licensed under
the [Apache License 2.0](./LICENSE.md). **Trademarks are different from
copyright.** As stated in Section 6 of the Apache License 2.0, an open-source
software license grants rights in the *code* — it does **not** grant any right
to use Apiboost's names or logos. This policy governs use of the marks.

## 2. Marks covered by this policy

- **Apiboost®** — a registered trademark of Apiboost, Inc.
- **Apiboost OmniSpec** — a product name and trademark of Apiboost, Inc.
- The **Apiboost logo** and any associated design marks.

Collectively, these are referred to here as the "Apiboost Marks."

## 3. Uses that are always allowed (nominative / referential use)

You do **not** need permission to make truthful, referential use of the Apiboost
Marks, including to:

- State, accurately, that your product is built on, integrates with, is
  compatible with, or extends Apiboost OmniSpec
  (e.g., "Powered by Apiboost OmniSpec", "compatible with Apiboost OmniSpec").
- Refer to the software by name in documentation, articles, blog posts, talks,
  tutorials, academic papers, and news coverage.
- Use the word marks in unmodified plain text where reasonably necessary to
  describe the origin of the software, as contemplated by the NOTICE file and
  Section 6 of the Apache License 2.0.

When you make referential use, please: use the marks only as adjectives (not as
your own product name), follow the correct spelling and capitalization
("Apiboost", "Apiboost OmniSpec"), and do not imply sponsorship, endorsement, or
affiliation that does not exist.

## 4. Uses that require prior written permission

You must obtain prior written permission from Apiboost before you:

- Use any Apiboost Mark in the **name of your product, service, company,
  domain name, social media account, or application** (for example, naming a
  fork or a plugin "Apiboost OmniSpec Plus" or "Apiboost-Docs-Pro").
- Use the Apiboost logo or any design mark, or a confusingly similar mark.
- Use an Apiboost Mark in a way that suggests Apiboost sponsors, endorses,
  certifies, or is affiliated with your product, service, or event.
- Sell merchandise bearing an Apiboost Mark.

## 5. Uses that are not permitted

You may not:

- Use an Apiboost Mark, or a confusingly similar mark, as the name of a
  **modified version or fork** you distribute. If you distribute a modified
  version of the open-source core, you must give it a distinct name that does not
  include the Apiboost Marks, and you must comply with the modification-notice
  requirements of the Apache License 2.0. You may still truthfully state that
  your software is "based on Apiboost OmniSpec."
- Use the Apiboost Marks in a manner that is misleading, defamatory, infringes
  any right, or violates any applicable law.
- Register or attempt to register any Apiboost Mark, or any confusingly similar
  mark or domain name, anywhere in the world.
- Incorporate the Apiboost Marks into your own trademarks, logos, or company
  names.

## 6. Forks and derivative works

The Apache License 2.0 gives you broad rights to fork and modify the open-source
core. It does not give you rights to the Apiboost Marks. A fork or derivative
work must be released under a **different name and branding** that does not use
or imply the Apiboost Marks, except for a truthful "based on" or "compatible
with" statement as described in Section 3.
